Hello, I’m Anbarasan, a NLP Research Engineer with a passion for natural language processing and deep learning. I do Applied Research using Deep Learning to solve challenging problems in Natural Language Processing and Understanding (NLP/NLU) to support Information Extraction (IE), Search and Information Retrieval (IR), and Chatbot platforms.I started this blog to share my knowledge and experience in these fields, and to help others learn about the latest advancements in NLP. I hope you'll join me on this journey and learn something new along the way. If you have any questions or want to connect, feel free to reach out to me via my contact page or on linkedin. Thanks for stopping by!
